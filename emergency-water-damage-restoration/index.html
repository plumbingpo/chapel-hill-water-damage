How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Insulate pipes in unheated spaces so they do not freeze and split in cold weather.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.

Why Hire a Professional for Emergency Water Damage Restoration?

The visible water is the easy part of an emergency. The difficulty lies in tracking where the remaining water travelled.
